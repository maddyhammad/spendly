Data We Collect

Spendly gathers information you voluntarily provide, such as your email, password, and the details of expenses you enter (amount, category, date, notes). We do not collect any financial account numbers, bank credentials, or personal identification numbers.

How We Use Your Data

The data you give us is only used to power the Spendly service – storing, retrieving, and displaying your expense records. We do not sell, trade, or otherwise share your data with third parties without your explicit consent, except as required by law.

Data Storage

Your data is stored in a secure SQLite database hosted on our server. We back it up regularly and employ encryption for data at rest. Access is limited to authorized personnel and the application itself.

Third‑Party Services

We may use third‑party services (e.g., hosting, email notifications) that comply with privacy best practices. Their privacy policies apply to those services, and we recommend reviewing them for more detail.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us at privacy@spendly.com.
