  • Privacy Policy

  • Information We Collect

    We collect personal details such as your name, email, and financial transaction data to provide the core functionality of Spendly.

  • How We Use Information

    Your data is used exclusively to generate spending insights, category breakdowns, and to improve the overall user experience.

  • User‑Provided Financial/Expense Data

    All expense entries, charts, and reports are stored locally in our database and are only accessible to the logged‑in user.

  • Data Storage and Security

    Data is stored in a secured PostgreSQL database (or SQLite in dev) with encryption at rest and TLS in transit. Only authorized personnel have access.

  • Cookies and Similar Technologies

    We use session cookies to manage authentication. No tracking cookies are set for analytics.

  • Third‑Party Services

    We do not share data with third‑party services except for backend services required for payment or email verification. No third‑party analytics are deployed.

  • Data Retention

    Expense data remains in our database until the user deletes their account or explicitly removes data. We retain aggregated data for analytics only for a maximum of 12 months.

  • User Rights

    Users can request deletion, correction, or export of their data via the account settings page. Users may also opt‑out of data usage for analytics.

  • Children's Privacy

    Spendly is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

  • Changes to This Privacy Policy

    We reserve the right to update this policy. Updates will be posted here and take effect immediately upon posting.

  • Contact Information

    If you have questions or concerns, contact us at support@spendly.com.
